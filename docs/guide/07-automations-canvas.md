# To arrange steps on the Automations canvas

Open **Automations**, choose **New** or open a saved automation, then use the canvas to place and connect its steps.

Drag a step to move it. Steps snap into place on a grid to help keep the canvas tidy. Choose **Arrange steps** to lay them out automatically. Use the minimap to see where you are in a large automation and move around it.

## To start from a pictured template

Open **Templates** to browse examples with preview pictures. Select a template to see what it needs and what it will produce. Choose **Use this template** to make a copy you can review and change on the canvas.
