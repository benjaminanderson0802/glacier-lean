# To review a claim

## What you will do

You will review a claim: a saved report about a problem, missing ability or unclear instruction that Glacier could not settle alone.

## What you need

You need a claim waiting for review. It may include a summary, research and evidence. Read those details before choosing what should happen.

## Steps

1. Open **Home** and look in **Needs you** for a claim to review. You can also choose **all claims**.
2. Open the claim and read **What research found** and **Proof**. A claim keeps the problem visible while it is investigated; it is not proof that the proposed solution is right.
3. Choose **Approve** if the proposal and evidence make sense, **Reject** if you do not accept it, or **Ask for more research** if you need better answers.
4. If the claim includes a run, choose **View run** to see its steps and results.

## If something goes wrong

If you see **No research notes yet.**, there may not be research to review. If the claim says **Research is still running.**, wait and check again. If there is no proof, the panel says **No evidence attached.** Do not approve a proposal you cannot assess; ask for more research or reject it.

## What to try next

After the claim is settled, return to **Home** to review other items waiting for you. For details on a run's result, read [Your first automation from a template](01-first-automation.md).
