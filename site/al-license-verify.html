For teams that repeatedly check subcontractors

Alabama contractor roster checks

Compare exact license numbers or business names against a dated Alabama contractor roster CSV your team is authorized to use. Results include the roster date and literal status and expiration fields when present.

Unvalidated pilot offer: $49 for up to 100 checks

What you provide

  • One roster CSV that you are authorized to provide and use.
  • Up to 100 exact license-number or business-name checks.
  • The source and date for the roster.

What you receive

A dated CSV or JSON report with exact matches, source date, and the status and expiration fields present in your file. A missing match or uncertain input is labeled accordingly.

Your responsibilities

Confirm important records directly with the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors and decide what they mean for your work. You remain responsible for qualification decisions, signatures, and filings. This is not legal advice.

Limits

The tool reads only the file you provide; it does not browse the board site. A roster is a dated snapshot, not real-time status. A match is not proof of identity, legal qualification, insurance, workmanship, or endorsements.
