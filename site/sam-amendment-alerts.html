SAM change digest pilot

Compare dated SAM.gov opportunity snapshots for one agreed NAICS code and show newly observed notices, version changes, and response-deadline changes with source links.

Proposed: $49 once for four weeks

Live collection is not active in this pilot kit. A start date can be offered only after the official data connection works, required checks pass, and two businesses agree. No charge is automatic.

What you provide

  • The agreed six-digit NAICS code and watch scope.
  • For a pilot, the necessary opportunity preferences and review of a real sample after the data connection is ready.

What you receive

A dated digest of new notices and observed version or response-deadline changes between saved snapshots, with official notice links and retrieval times.

Your responsibilities

Check each live notice and deadline on SAM.gov before acting. Decide whether an opportunity fits your business and handle all bid decisions, signatures, and filings yourself. This is not legal or procurement advice.

Limits

The prototype compares saved snapshots; live snapshot capture and scheduling are not implemented. It cannot recover changes from before monitoring starts, and it does not promise every amendment, attachment change, cancellation, or opportunity.
