Public-works bid triage

Turn public-works notices your team already received into a short, source-linked weekly list for your selected trade and counties. The initial pilot is for Mobile and Baldwin Counties.

$49 once for 30 days · four weekly digests

The pilot proceeds only if three firms commit and a contractor finds a useful match or time saved from a permitted, current sample. No automatic renewal.

What you provide

  • Official notice emails, exports, or links that your team may share.
  • Your desired trade and counties.
  • A review of the sample and agreed scope before a start date.

What you receive

Four weekly digests listing issuer, county, listed deadline, pre-bid details when supplied, official source link, and a review step. Missing fields remain uncertain.

Your responsibilities

Verify live notices, addenda, deadlines, eligibility, and bid instructions with the issuing authority. You handle vendor registration and prepare, sign, and submit your own bids. This is not procurement or legal advice.

Limits

This service triages notices you supply; it does not search procurement sites, provide complete market coverage, contact agencies, register vendors, or submit bids. Official portal search and alerts are free alternatives.
