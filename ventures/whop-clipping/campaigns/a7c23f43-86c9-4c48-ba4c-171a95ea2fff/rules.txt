SUPPLIED SETUP/WARM-UP GUIDE (campaign instructions, not independently verified claims about algorithms):
Create on the same iPhone used for recordings; same phone/Wi-Fi daily, no country-changing VPN. Set country/language to United States at creation; use own real email, add phone when asked, enable 2FA, save password securely. Make accounts on separate days (one, wait a day, then next), never five at once. No rapid switching among six accounts or repeated device/network/country changes.
Short pronounceable aviation name; no terminal digits, underscores or dots; if taken change word, not spelling. Do not impersonate app, use official/real/hq adjacent to app name, copy another clipper's name or use unrelated name. ATC forbids "ATC Live"/"ATC App"; FlightDeck setup forbids plain "FlightDeck"/"FlightDeck App". Use plane/aviation photo, not app logo; unique name/photo/bio for each account.
Initially short bio: what page covers + posting frequency, third line empty; no access code, words ad/sponsor/partner, emoji clutter, "DM me"/"click here" or early link. Wait a few hours after completing profile before warm-up. Main brief's required app mention/download CTA must be reconciled with setup bio guidance before posting.
Warm-up daily 10–15 minutes: watch aviation-only videos (vary completion/swipes), like 5–10, write 1–2 real full-sentence unique comments, save 1–2, share 1, follow 2–3. Spread actions out, varying time of day; not all in 2 minutes. Only aviation/US airport and pilot content, not unrelated food/cars/games. Never mass follow/like, copy comments, buy engagement or fake interactions. Research does not perform these account actions.
YouTube/TikTok warm-up 3–5 days; Instagram 7 FULL days. Zero posts during warm-up, not even test clips or same-day creation posts. Main briefs suggest about a week and a few posts; the separate guide is stricter: no warm-up posts. Prepare edits meanwhile.
After warm-up wait a few hours, then one clip/day in week 2; week 3+ only 2–3/day if healthy. Suggested US windows 12–2pm ET or 6–10pm ET; choose one consistently. Continue 5 minutes/day viewing/engagement after posting starts. This differs from the venture's normal six-hour schedule: do not automatically enroll these campaigns.
Add YouTube/IG bio link AFTER first post; TikTok only when link setting becomes available (guide says approximately 1K followers); until then no link. Use required bio text fallback where applicable. Each post: 3–5 hashtags, own edit, no other-app watermark, change caption and first frame per account. Final checklist's generic "No watermark" conflicts with mandatory campaign watermark; retain mandatory brand/end-card watermark and exclude other-app marks.
