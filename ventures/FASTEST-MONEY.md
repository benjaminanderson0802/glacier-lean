## Why these three, and what would change the ranking

One $49 direct sale is materially more useful for first cash than hundreds of one-cent checks. Job closeout has a concrete artifact a shop can inspect without government filing or a marketplace review. Its main weakness is customers already having adequate reports; reject that buyer rather than adding features. At $49, ten packets yield only $4.90 gross each: one repeatable template and a cooperative customer are essential.

Alabama triage can operate from authorized supplied notices today, but requiring three commitments makes it harder than a one-buyer offer. Keep that rule; no fake “found bids” from the fixture. SAM has a similarly bounded output but needs a live data path first. It ranks third for prospect discovery and conditional interest, not immediate invoicing.

Current [API snapshots and all competitor charge events](apify-tools/PRICING.md) show parseforge's California license Actor at 1,453 lifetime runs / 104 users / 6 recent users, and pink_comic's at 3,953 / 60 / 14. The adjacent recall matcher has 56 / 2 / 1. These are weak signals for near-term paid demand, not buyer counts. Search counts are noisy and not exhaustive. This is why expanding the Actor catalog loses to testing a $49 offer.

Move carpenter goods above the pilots only if Benjamin already has signed commission terms, stock ready for pickup and a likely buyer. Move an Actor up for earned revenue if an independent paying user arrives; keep its payout timing unchanged. Remove a pilot if customer review requires Benjamin to spend >10 minutes/day or if its current software cannot deliver the stated scope.

## Stop rules and first-dollar accounting

- **Immediately:** no authorized source/data path, false source claim, uncertain identity field, buyer opt-out or unresolved operating permission → no affected send/delivery/order. No paid ads, postcards, subscriptions, scraping restricted portals or money spent to manufacture demand.
- **Closeout:** preserve the first-five accuracy/usefulness gate; repeated corrections or no time saved means no payment. No paying pilot by day 14 (or 10 qualified invitations) → park acquisition and extra feature work.
- **Alabama:** fewer than three explicit $49 commitments or no demonstrated usefulness → no launch/invoice; reassess after day 14. Do not lower this gate silently to claim first revenue.
- **SAM:** no two explicit $49 commitments, no verified current snapshots, or no lawful collection path → no sale. Day 14 with no ready buyer/live path → park; existing hard maximum remains 15 invitations/30 days.
- **Apify:** source/canary failure or unclear permissions → block publication/sales; after 14 public days with zero independent paid use, stop adding Actors. Keep verification and maintenance within the daily budget; no guaranteed payout forecast.
- **Time/margin:** if two fulfillment attempts cannot stay within 10 owner minutes/day, stop onboarding. At $49, no bespoke integrations or multi-round consultations. Record actual Glacier operating cost as well as cash margin; a sale at a loss is not a sustainable business.

Success at day 14 is **one settled $49 pilot payment with checked delivery**, not all ventures launched. A realistic outcome is $0 with useful rejection evidence. Nothing in this plan authorizes the agent to send, post, publish, sign up, pay, log in or push.
