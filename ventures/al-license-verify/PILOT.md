## Paid pilot offer

Unvalidated offer hypothesis: **$49 for one customer-supplied roster and up to 100 exact checks**, delivered as a dated CSV/JSON report. No buyer has validated this price or offer. The customer supplies a roster file it is authorized to use. Results are point-in-time matches only; the customer confirms important records directly with the board. Do not promise a current status determination, identity confirmation, legal clearance, insurance, or contractor quality. The future Apify price hypothesis is $0.05 per lookup, capped at $5 per run; it is not enabled by this pilot draft.

The municipal directory establishes public organization and contact-route provenance only. It does not establish that any listed office buys this service, checks contractor licenses, or has this workflow. Treat `prospects.csv` as an unvalidated research list, not qualified buyers.

## Source permission owner step

Before any board-sourced roster is collected, redistributed, or sold against, Benjamin asks the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors: **Is there a downloadable roster/export for commercial use, and does the board permit automated processing and commercial redistribution of that export and its status/expiration fields?** Record the answer and source terms before enabling any board data path. The search pages themselves are not automated by this Actor; the board's own displayed disclaimer warns its public database may be incomplete and not official. Until the owner has an answer, use only a buyer-supplied file whose use the buyer is authorized to permit.

## Stop rule

No outreach, listing, or payment is part of this draft. Any later outreach is an owner decision and requires independently checking the current public contact route and whether the workflow is relevant. Do not proceed with a roster unless the buyer confirms they may provide it. This draft does not establish a buyer-fit result or a time-based stopping threshold. Do not publish an Apify listing or spend money as part of this pilot draft.
