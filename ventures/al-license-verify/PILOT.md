# Alabama roster verifier pilot

## Email Benjamin can send

**Subject:** Would a dated subcontractor license roster check save your team time?

Hello,

I’m testing a small Alabama contractor roster checker for teams that repeatedly verify subcontractors. It compares exact license numbers or business names against a dated roster CSV your team is authorized to use and returns the source date with each result. It does not browse the board site or make a legal qualification decision.

Would you be willing to try one paid pilot using an export your organization is allowed to share? I’m testing whether this saves enough staff time to be useful. If it does not fit your process, a quick “no” is helpful too.

Benjamin Anderson<br>
Anderson Education LLC

## Paid pilot offer

Hypothesis: **$49 for one customer-supplied roster and up to 100 exact checks**, delivered as a dated CSV/JSON report. The customer supplies a roster file it is authorized to use. Results are point-in-time matches only; the customer confirms important records directly with the board. Do not promise a current status determination, identity confirmation, legal clearance, insurance, or contractor quality. The future Apify hypothesis is $0.05 per lookup, capped at $5 per run.

## Source permission owner step

Before any board-sourced roster is collected, redistributed, or sold against, Benjamin asks the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors: **Is there a downloadable roster/export for commercial use, and does the board permit automated processing and commercial redistribution of that export and its status/expiration fields?** Record the answer and source terms before enabling any board data path. The search pages themselves are not automated by this Actor; the board's own displayed disclaimer warns its public database may be incomplete and not official. Until the owner has an answer, use only a buyer-supplied file whose use the buyer is authorized to permit.

## Stop rule

Send the pitch to the 15 municipal offices in `prospects.csv`. Stop this experiment after 14 days if nobody agrees to a paid pilot, or immediately if the buyer cannot provide a roster it is authorized to share. Do not publish an Apify listing or spend money as part of this pilot draft.
