## Georgia ST-5 handling

Georgia DOR says a Georgia business may provide a properly completed [Form ST-5](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/form/st-5-sales-tax-certificate-exemption/download) to a supplier to purchase tangible personal property for resale without paying Georgia sales tax. For a resale certificate, the DOR says the purchaser must be engaged in selling tangible personal property and have a valid sales-tax registration number entered on the certificate. The ST-5 is a supplier-specific certificate: the supplier's name/address and purchaser's actual registration and signature details must be completed truthfully. It is not a blank substitute for registration, and it does not cover inventory the purchaser uses or consumes. [DOR resale guidance](https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/business-taxes/sales-use-tax/nontaxable-sales).

Mammoth's dealer form asks whether a resale/sales-tax permit is on file and says it is required to buy for resale tax-free. Select `No` unless the actual permit is already issued; do not select `Yes` based on a pending application. Check the current Georgia DOR dealer/registration guidance and complete any required registration personally before using ST-5. For suppliers that do not publish an ST-5 requirement, ask; do not send a signed certificate unless the resale purchase qualifies and the form is complete.
