## Offer

- Four weeks, one NAICS watch (starting with 423840), and one weekly dated digest.
- $49 total for the four-week pilot; no automatic renewal.
- Notices link to SAM.gov. The customer checks the live solicitation and all requirements.
- A live pilot starts only after the owner has a free, owner-provided SAM.gov API key and confirms current source terms and rate limits.
- The sample in this repository is fixture based; it must not be represented as a live source result.

## Stop rule

Stop after 15 individually reviewed invitations or 30 days, whichever comes first. Continue only if at least two businesses explicitly agree to the paid pilot at $49. If fewer than two agree, do not build more acquisition or monitoring features for this offer; record the result and reassess the idea. Do not send bulk email or follow up with anyone who declines or asks not to be contacted.
