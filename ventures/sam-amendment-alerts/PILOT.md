**Before sale:** two businesses must explicitly agree to the price; owner must provide an authorized SAM API key through Glacier Secrets; current terms/rate limits must permit collection; the team must capture and compare two real snapshots and verify pagination/coverage. **Delivery/acceptance:** agree the scope and start date after a real sample review. First digest within two business days after verified snapshot pair, then weekly (proposed target). Buyer confirms links open and the change summary is useful. If the source is stale or unavailable, mark the digest uncertain and suspend delivery; extend the period or refund $12.25 per missed weekly digest at the buyer's choice.

**Payment/data:** Benjamin sends a $49 invoice due on receipt through an existing business payment route after gates and start-date acceptance. No credentials by email, no agency filing, no bid writing or award assurance. SAM registration/CAGE for Benjamin's supply venture is not the product's data feed.
