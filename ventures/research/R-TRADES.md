# R-TRADES — paperwork automation for home-service businesses

**Research date:** 2026-10-10. **Scope:** low-capital, owner-light ventures that could start with Benjamin's Jobber developer, Apify, Shopify Partner, Chrome Web Store and SAM.gov-in-progress accounts. Costs below are launch cash estimates for a narrow pilot; they exclude the value of Benjamin's time, taxes, payment processing on sales, and any customer-specific paid portal/API. Revenue and conversion are unproven.

## Drift check / acceptance

- **Roadmap:** PH12, Ventures on Glacier; this report helps select work but does not itself complete a checkpoint. PH12 depends on PH5 and PH9, which NORTHSTAR lists as complete. The owner approved PH12 on 2026-10-10.
- **Properties/metrics:** P-GOALS and P-LOOPS; M-INTERVENE (target: fewer owner interventions) and M-TTFA (under 30 minutes to a first working automation).
- **Existing tools:** Jobber and Housecall Pro already provide core field-service management and some integrations. This research does not propose replacing them; it looks for narrow paperwork workflows. Jobber has a public API/marketplace and states 300,000+ home-service pros; its marketplace requires app review. Housecall Pro's public API is limited to MAX accounts. [Jobber developer docs](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/), [Jobber review process](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/publishing_your_app/app_review_process/), [Housecall Pro API overview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8505035-api-overview)
- **Acceptance:** 8–12 distinct ideas, top-three scoring across the requested factors, five concrete launch steps per top idea using existing accounts, and red flags; external factual claims have primary-source links. Estimates and judgments are labeled as such.

## Market signal and channel constraints

The addressable field-service base is substantial: the 2023 Census County Business Patterns lists 111,207 employer establishments in plumbing, heating and air-conditioning contracting, plus 25,519 roofing contractor establishments. These are establishment counts, not paying-customer estimates; Census excludes nonemployer firms from this table. [Census 238220](https://data.census.gov/profile/238220_-_Plumbing%2C_heating%2C_and_air-conditioning_contractors?codeset=naics~238220&g=010XX00US), [Census 238160](https://data.census.gov/table/CBP2023.CB2300CBP?codeset=naics~238160)

Jobber's developer documentation advertises access to 300,000+ home-service pros, OAuth scopes and a marketplace review process. The marketplace is a potential distribution channel, not a guaranteed approval or customer source. Jobber says draft apps connecting to more than five paying accounts can have API access blocked, so a direct pilot should stay within its custom-integration rules or proceed through review. [Jobber developer docs](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/), [custom integration limits](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/custom_integrations/)

Housecall Pro is a secondary route, but its API requires a MAX plan and its help page says it does not provide dedicated support for custom API requests. Start with Jobber or a customer-authorized manual CSV workflow; do not assume Housecall Pro API access is available to each prospect. [Housecall Pro API overview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8505035-api-overview)

Jobber's public app directory already includes broad operational categories and products such as reporting, e-signature, field-photo documentation and marketing integrations. This signals real app competition and incumbent feature overlap; it does not establish that a dedicated product below has no competitors. Verify the live marketplace by hand before applying. [Jobber App Marketplace directory](https://help.getjobber.com/en/topics/app-marketplace/)

### Launch plan 1 — job closeout packet builder

1. **Define a narrow packet** for one trade (HVAC installs first): job summary, approved photos, model/serial, invoice and customer handoff checklist. Use the Jobber developer account/API with the minimum OAuth scopes; Jobber admins authorize the connection. [Jobber OAuth](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/building_your_app/app_authorization/)
2. **Build a no-cost local prototype** that accepts Jobber-exported job data plus an explicitly selected folder of photos, produces a PDF and flags missing fields. Do not scrape Jobber or upload customer documents to an unapproved service.
3. **Recruit one pilot shop** through an existing relationship/direct introduction; manually review ten packets against source records. Charge nothing until the shop confirms the packet saves time and the contents are accurate.
4. **Offer a direct paid pilot** with a simple one-time setup or monthly fee only after value is demonstrated; use the owner-configured billing capability if available, otherwise invoice manually. The owner approves pricing and terms once.
5. **Submit a Jobber marketplace app** after internal testing, using the existing developer account; request review and follow the required OAuth disconnect and listing requirements. Add Housecall Pro only later, since its API is MAX-plan gated. [Jobber review](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/publishing_your_app/app_review_process/), [Housecall Pro API](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8505035-api-overview)

**Owner actions:** approve the packet schema and first pilot; approve marketplace submission (two). **First revenue estimate:** 1–3 weeks via direct pilot; marketplace time is longer and uncertain. **Red flags:** existing CompanyCam/reporting integrations; photo consent; false impression that the packet is legal/inspection certification; marketplace review not guaranteed.

### Launch plan 2 — one-territory rebate packet preparation

1. **Pick one utility territory by owner decision** where a specific utility/state incentive has current official eligibility, forms and a stable program page. No nationwide rules engine initially. Use ENERGY STAR's rebate finder and the program administrator's own site as discovery pointers, then cite the official program source in each packet. [ENERGY STAR](https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome)
2. **Create a checklist** from the program's current official rules: equipment/model, installer credentials if required, invoice, installation date and customer consent. The tool returns “match / no match found in source as of date / uncertain,” not an eligibility guarantee.
3. **Build a Jobber-connected or CSV-first intake** using Benjamin's Jobber developer account; customer or contractor supplies the data and reviews every field. Avoid automated portal submission until the program expressly allows the method.
4. **Run five historical or synthetic cases** with one HVAC contractor; compare each generated packet against its source documents and record corrections. This is a research/validation step, not a promise of award success.
5. **Sell a fixed-price pilot per packet or a small shop subscription** by direct outreach; after proof, apply to the Jobber marketplace. Do not advertise tax advice or success rates. [Jobber review](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/publishing_your_app/app_review_process/)

**Owner actions:** select territory and approve first checklist; approve the first customer-facing description (two). **First revenue estimate:** 2–6 weeks. **Red flags:** state program availability and rules vary; application flows can change; rebates may be point-of-sale rather than paperwork; no guarantee of eligibility or payment. Federal tax-credit dates change: ENERGY STAR currently states the cited central-air tax credit applied only to purchases/installs through 2025, so do not pitch a 2026 federal tax credit from stale pages. [ENERGY STAR credit page](https://www.energystar.gov/about/federal-tax-credits/central-air-conditioners)

### Launch plan 3 — HVAC warranty registration and deadline ledger

1. **Choose one OEM and verify its terms** for contractor registration, delegated access, data retention and automated entry. If terms do not clearly permit automation, productize document prep/reminders and let the customer submit.
2. **Use the Jobber developer account** to read completed install jobs through OAuth; request only required scopes. Start with CSV/photo upload if API access or marketplace review would slow the first test. [Jobber OAuth](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/building_your_app/app_authorization/)
3. **Parse model/serial from the installer’s equipment photo**, cross-check with a second engine and a deterministic format check; disagreements become “uncertain—please check.” Match each OEM deadline from that OEM's current official warranty page.
4. **Pilot with one shop and ten installations**; the contractor reviews names, addresses and equipment data and authorizes each registration. Save the OEM confirmation and prevent duplicate registration.
5. **Charge a small fixed monthly pilot** after confirming reduced missed deadlines; then request Jobber app review. Only add other OEMs after separate terms and workflow validation. [Jobber review](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/publishing_your_app/app_review_process/)

**Owner actions:** choose/approve the first OEM and authorize its portal policy; approve first marketplace submission (two). **First revenue estimate:** 2–6 weeks by direct pilot; OEM approval and app listing may extend this. **Red flags:** not every brand makes registration a condition of warranty (Bosch explicitly says its eligibility is unaffected); OEM rules vary; portals may prohibit bots; customer personal data; duplicate or inaccurate registration can harm customer trust. [Bosch](https://www.bosch-homecomfort.com/us/en/residential/service-support/product-warranty-registration-information/), [Carrier](https://www.carrier.com/us/en/residential/homeowner-resources/warranty/)

## Rejected or deferred approaches

- **Scrape logged-in marketplaces, Facebook/LinkedIn, load boards, or a manufacturer/customer portal without permission:** reject. The Venture Build Spec requires official APIs and approved integrations first and prohibits logged-in consumer-platform scraping; use customer-authorized Jobber OAuth, exports, and only sources whose terms allow automation.
- **Nationwide permit filing:** defer. Municipal rules and forms fragment the product, and automating filings creates representation/authorization risk. Start with packet prefill in a single jurisdiction only after checking official rules.
- **“Recover a rebate/tax credit” claims:** reject as initial positioning. Tax-credit program dates change and free lookup tools exist; do not sell legal/tax conclusions or imply a guaranteed award. [ENERGY STAR credit page](https://www.energystar.gov/about/federal-tax-credits/central-air-conditioners), [ENERGY STAR rebate finder](https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome)
- **OSHA filing as the broad lead offer:** defer. Construction employers may be covered depending on establishment size/industry, but small businesses under the threshold are out of scope; the employer owns and certifies the injury data, and the annual window makes revenue seasonal. [OSHA coverage](https://www.osha.gov/itareportapp)

## Recommendation

Test the **job closeout packet builder** first: its output can be prepared and independently checked without making legal judgments or submitting through third-party portals. Validate willingness to pay with one direct pilot before investing in marketplace approval. Keep **one-territory rebate packets** as second choice if a local HVAC shop confirms the paperwork is a recurring pain. Treat warranty registration as viable only brand-by-brand after the portal terms are verified; one OEM's workflow cannot stand in for all brands.

No candidate currently has proven customer willingness to pay. An owner interview/pilot and current marketplace competitor review are the first decision gates; the scores are prioritization hypotheses only.
