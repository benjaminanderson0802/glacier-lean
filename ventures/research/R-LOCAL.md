## Read this before choosing

There is real public procurement information in the target region: Alabama Builds publishes public-works notices, and its current listing includes municipalities, counties, public universities and authorities; Alabama points vendors to Alabama Buys for non-construction state procurement. [Alabama Builds](https://alabamabuilds.gov/), [Alabama bid advertisements](https://alabamabuilds.gov/AdvertisementBids), [Alabama Vendor Self Service](https://procurement.staars.alabama.gov/PRDVSS1X1/AltSelfService?DepartmentCode=087&Destination=pSolication&DocumentCode=RFP&DocumentID=24000000005&openDoc=openDoc). Georgia DOAS describes the Georgia Procurement Registry (GPR) as public solicitation listings and says local governments use it; suppliers must register to submit bids. [Georgia DOAS supplier guide](https://www.doas.ga.gov/state-purchasing/information-for-suppliers-and-ga-businesses), [GPR local-government guidance](https://www.doas.ga.gov/state-purchasing/information-for-local-governments-and-municipalities).

Do not confuse this evidence of available procurements with proof that a new vendor-alert business has paying customers. Free official search, saved-search alerts and established paid aggregators already exist. For example, SAM.gov offers saved-search email notifications, and GovTribe lists paid plans at $1,900/year and $6,000/year. A viable product needs a narrow local specialty and better actionable filtering, not another all-purpose bid board. [SAM.gov help](https://sam.gov/help), [GovTribe pricing](https://govtribe.com/plans).

For federal work, SAM.gov is the official place to find opportunities and award data; its public Opportunity API and public downloads are usable sources. The site explicitly prohibits automated data gathering and web scraping, and limits public sharing to data from public API versions. **Do not scrape SAM.gov pages or use a sensitive API for a public-facing product.** [SAM.gov contracting](https://sam.gov/contracting), [GSA public opportunities API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/), [SAM.gov terms](https://sam.gov/about/terms-of-use).

The ownerΓÇÖs SAM registration is not an instant set-aside advantage. A registered firm may represent small-business status only where it meets the relevant NAICS size standard and solicitation rules. SBA lists additional eligibility and certification requirements for 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB/EDWOSB and SDVOSB; none should be claimed without independently meeting them and obtaining any required certification. SBA states SAM registration is required to participate as a federal prime. [SBA contracting basics](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/get-started/), [SBA certifications](https://www.sba.gov/certifications/), [SBA prime and subcontracting](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/prime-and-subcontracting/). No facts supplied establish eligibility for socioeconomic set-asides.

AlabamaΓÇÖs state OMBE certification is limited to qualifying minority- and/or women-owned entities and requires, among other things, two years in business under qualifying ownership and legal presence/licensing in Alabama. The ownerΓÇÖs residence alone does not establish eligibility, and no ownership facts were provided to infer it. [ADECA OMBE requirements](https://adeca.alabama.gov/ombe/). Georgia accepts suppliers through its procurement registration process, but local business preferences or certifications must not be inferred from a Georgia LLC alone. [Georgia supplier registration](https://www.doas.ga.gov/state-purchasing/information-for-suppliers-and-ga-businesses).

One Alabama-specific issue matters to any paid bid-alert concept: Alabama administrative rules say the Division of Purchasing will not accept a vendor registration from a ΓÇ£bid subscription organization.ΓÇ¥ Keep the product as customer-side research/alerts; each contractor should control its own vendor profile, commodity codes, credentials, and bid submission. Confirm the current interpretation with Alabama procurement counsel or the agency before offering any registration or representation service. [Alabama Administrative Code, vendor requirements](https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us/administrative-code/355-4-1-.03). Alabama Buys itself tells suppliers to select commodity codes to get email notifications, which is a free substitute the product must beat through focus and context. [Alabama VSS/Alabama Buys portal](https://procurement.staars.alabama.gov/PRDVSS1X1/AltSelfService?DepartmentCode=087&Destination=pSolication&DocumentCode=RFP&DocumentID=24000000005&openDoc=openDoc).

### 1) Alabama public-works bid triage ΓÇö recommended first experiment

**Five-step launch plan (owner actions marked):**

## Other viable tests and explicit rejects

- **Georgia Export Grant packet assistant:** worth a customer discovery interview only if a Georgia-based exporter will pay for paperwork preparation. The grant is competitive, requires preapproval, reimburses approved expenses after the business incurs them, and requires the company to operate in Georgia and meet export-readiness requirements; the ownerΓÇÖs Georgia LLC alone does not establish those facts. [Georgia grant details](https://georgia.org/business-support/export-growth/georgia-export-grant), [eligibility FAQ](https://georgia.org/business-support/export-growth/georgia-export-grant/FAQ).
- **Apify Actor distribution:** reasonable as a later channel for a permissioned data transformer or API wrapper. Apify allows pay-per-event and pay-per-usage monetization, but marketplace presence is not evidence of demand; price against actual compute/platform costs and confirm data rights first. [Apify monetization docs](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize), [Apify pricing](https://apify.com/pricing).
- **Jobber HVAC reminder app:** defer until customer interviews show a missing workflow. Jobber says its ecosystem reaches over 200,000 home-service pros, but existing marketplace apps compete; do not automate manufacturer warranty portals without permission. [Jobber marketplace](https://getjobber.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360062128653-App-Marketplace), [Jobber developer center](https://developer.getjobber.com/).
- **DIBBS resale:** reject as a first <$500/mostly-automated venture. A DLA quote can obligate accurate sourcing and fulfillment; this is inventory/working-capital and operational risk, not just an automated listing. [DIBBS](https://www.dibbs.bsm.dla.mil/).
- **Any product based on scraping SAM.gov or a private portal against its terms:** reject. SAMΓÇÖs official terms expressly prohibit automated data gathering/scraping. The same rule applies to other sites if their terms disallow it; no workaround through Apify, a browser extension, or Glacier changes the source terms. [SAM.gov terms](https://sam.gov/about/terms-of-use).

## Recommendation

Run the **Alabama public-works bid triage** experiment first: it has the clearest local public-source fit and the lowest incremental cost, but only after a customer interview validates a trade-specific gap over free official notices and alerts. Keep phase 1 as source-linked research and reminders. Do not offer bid filing, certification claims, legal advice, or guaranteed completeness. If source terms do not permit the desired ingestion, stay with official email notices, user-supplied links and exports rather than scraping. If fewer than three target firms commit to a paid pilot after the sample, stop and test the Georgia or federal concept instead.
