# Depop resale sourcing and policy research

**Checked:** 2026-10-11. **Geography/price example:** US, USD. Sources below are official Depop or Goodwill pages. Trend mentions are directional demand signals; they are not independent sell-through measurements, a guarantee, or a forecast of this owner's results.

## Current marketplace policy and crosslisting

- Depop's current [Terms of Service](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001773148-Terms-of-Service) (effective 2026-09-02) prohibit bots, scraping/crawling, and third-party software on the service, with an express allowance for third-party cross-listing platforms subject to complying with the Terms, promptly removing an item if it sells on an alternative platform, and accepting responsibility for double sales. The Terms also say not to offer items you do not possess, except personally designed items. This project only prepares local drafts for already-owned inventory and does not call Depop.
- Depop's [Dropshipping Policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/22499808769553-Dropshipping-Policy) disallows catalogue drop-shipped goods, including designer/luxury and cosmetic items, supplier/customer/stock images, and large-scale catalogue products bought to order. It requires original photos for the limited seller-designed items it allows to be made to order. This venture is BUY-FIRST and uses photos the owner took of the exact item.
- Depop's [Community Guidelines](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360026370634-Community-Guidelines) require honest descriptions and original images, ban stock photos and copied listings, and caution against irrelevant hashtags. Draft text uses supplied facts and restrained tags; the owner checks all output.
- Depop's [business/API guidance](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4411154329233-Selling-as-a-charity-or-business) says its API is not open to the public and requires contacting Depop for access; it also says Depop works with crosslisting tools through API connection. Depop's [API authentication documentation](https://partnerapi.depop.com/api-docs/concepts/authentication/) describes API keys for direct partners and OAuth for third-party applications such as crosslisting tools, and says to contact Depop to get access. **The public official pages reviewed here do not name approved vendors.** Therefore no named vendor can responsibly be asserted as approved from these sources. Only use a provider after the owner confirms its Depop API authorization; this tool has no vendor-specific schema or external integration.
- Depop's [USPS Shipping Policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11282781352465-USPS-Shipping-Policy-US) places packaging and dispatch responsibility on the seller, requires accurate parcel-size/address details, and says a Depop label is for the corresponding Depop order only. The checklist leaves order confirmation, label purchase, packing, dispatch, and tracking to the owner.
- The current [seller fee page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) shows no Depop selling fees for US sellers and US payment processing at 3.3% + $0.45 on sale price plus shipping and applicable taxes. A 12% boost fee applies if boosted listings are used. The ceilings assume no boosting and exclude tax from the simple model; the live fee page and sale breakdown control.

## Five category shortlist

Ranking is a practical shortlist by current official Depop trend fit plus ability to inspect and buy single pieces or small used lots. Depop publishes trend signals but no public category sell-through table sufficient to claim an exact sales ranking. All suggested purchases are existing used garments bought outright; inspect and obtain proof of purchase before listing. Avoid counterfeits, prohibited goods, supplier catalogue inventory, and any item not physically in stock.

Ceiling formula: `expected item sale × (1 − 0.50 target margin − 0.033 US processing rate) − $0.45 processing flat fee − seller-paid postage − $1.00 packaging`. The result is the maximum **all-in acquisition cost per sellable item**: item price plus its share of buyer's premium, inbound shipping, and acquisition taxes/fees. The table assumes seller-paid postage is `$0` (buyer-paid shipping or a Depop shipping label) and excludes owner labor, income taxes, returns, boosting, and other overhead. These are screening ceilings, not market prices. Replace the illustrative expected sale amount with a conservative owner-checked comparable before buying. Do not bid above the ceiling.

## Small-lot sourcing and purchase controls

- Goodwill Industries International's [shop-online page](https://www.goodwill.org/shoppers/shop-online/) says ShopGoodwill offers clothing from more than 150 regional Goodwills. The official [ShopGoodwill clothing listings](https://shopgoodwill.com/listing?catIds=&layout=grid) show single garments and lots; item examples include used denim and small apparel lots. Listings are time-sensitive and may be sold as-is. Inspect listing photos, contents, condition, shipping/handling, and lot restrictions; the current page is an example of a channel, not a recommendation to purchase a specific lot.
- Goodwill's [thrift-store locator](https://www.goodwill.org/locate-a-thrift-store/) helps find local retail stores and outlets. Other legitimate local thrift stores, consignment stores, estate sales, and direct private sellers can also sell one item at a time. Keep dated receipts or purchase records. Do not imply a local store guarantees resale rights beyond the ordinary sale; the owner must verify lawful title and product permissibility.
- The product strategy is resale of individually owned used goods, not liquidation of new catalogue products. A small used lot is only acceptable if the owner can inspect and take possession of each listed item, document its cost, and describe each piece honestly. Unknown contents, unverifiable designer/luxury claims, cosmetics, counterfeits, stock/supplier photos, and goods shipped directly from suppliers are excluded.

## Limits and review requirements

Trend relevance does not prove realized sales or profit. The $30–$42 example prices and 50% margin target are explicit screening assumptions, not external price claims. The output does not measure garment authenticity, legal resale rights, photo origin, Depop category mapping, shipping weight, tax, current competitor prices, or account eligibility. The owner checks current policy, item-specific facts, category/size fields, fees, price, and final listing before publication. No account action, listing, purchase, payment, label purchase, or Depop connection was performed for this research.

For stable source lookup, the official policy identifiers are `https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/22499808769553-dropshipping-policy`, `https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001773148-terms-of-service`, and `https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4411154329233-selling-as-a-charity-or-business`. The local tool has no Depop account access; its photo-origin check only requires the owner to declare `owner_original` and provide local files.
