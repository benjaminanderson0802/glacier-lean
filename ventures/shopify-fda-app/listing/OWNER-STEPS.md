# Owner steps — Shopify App Store submission

The copy and media are prepared in `STORE-LISTING.md`, `PRIVACY-POLICY.md`, `screenshots/`, `assets/`, and `review/`. Keep distribution in Draft until every non-owner release gate recorded in `ventures/fda-cosmetics/PROOF.md` is resolved.

## One Partner Dashboard session (about 30–60 minutes, plus deployment time)

1. **Publish the privacy page and complete real account values.** Open [andersoneducation.us](https://andersoneducation.us/) and publish `PRIVACY-POLICY.md` at `https://andersoneducation.us/privacy-policy`. Replace `{{BUSINESS_EMAIL}}` and the business-address placeholder with owner-confirmed contact details. Update the hosting-provider/location disclosure after choosing the app's production host. The domain and policy page were not reachable during preparation, so verify both from a logged-out browser.
2. **Deploy and configure the app.** Choose a no-spend HTTPS host, deploy this repository's `ventures/shopify-fda-app`, set `SHOPIFY_APP_URL` and `SHOPIFY_BILLING_TEST=false` only after live billing checks, then copy the base URL into `shopify.app.toml` and the Partner Dashboard configuration. Link the existing Partner app and `glacier-test-cosmetics` store; confirm reviewer access. No host, domain, email, reviewer access, or production deployment is configured by this package.
3. **Complete Shopify's review page.** Sign in at [Shopify Partners](https://partners.shopify.com/), open **Apps → Glacier FDA Listing → Distribution → Shopify App Store**, and fill the listing from `STORE-LISTING.md`. Paste the app introduction, details, features, search terms, category suggestion, and privacy URL exactly as written. Upload the prepared icon, feature image, three screenshots, and screencast. Choose and enter the actual prices (the $4.99 Starter and $9.99 Studio amounts are unapproved recommendations), add the real support and emergency contacts, opt out of protected customer data if the live app still does not access it, run the automated checks, then submit only after the app and venture release gates are cleared. Shopify's dashboard wording can change; follow its current App Store review page if labels differ.

## Each customer FDA filing (about 30 minutes per product after source facts are assembled)

Open [Shopify Admin](https://admin.shopify.com/) and review the exact product label, ingredient order, category, facility/FEI or confirmed exemption, responsible-person information, marketing/listing dates, and exemption answers. Run the local packet workflow in `ventures/fda-cosmetics/README.md`; compare every source and reminder with the actual label. If a listing is due, the responsible person opens [Cosmetics Direct](https://direct.fda.gov/), uploads the prepared SPL ZIP, signs where required, submits, and saves the returned response for the local parser. The time to gather brand records is unknown and varies by product.

The production URL, contact details, host and its data-location/retention facts, price decision, legal-policy approval, and publication are account-owner inputs. No account was created, no service was purchased, no app was published, and no submission was made.
