# UPS packet checklist

## Deadline

UPS says to start a claim within 60 days of the scheduled delivery date for a lost or damaged package. Treat this as a filing deadline estimate: confirm the shipment's service and current UPS terms, then retain the source and checked date in the packet.

**Date anchor:** scheduled delivery date. **Response:** save the claim number, submission confirmation, and every UPS notice. Do not assume a response date; follow up using the date UPS actually provides.

## Evidence

- Tracking number and shipment/order record
- Bill of lading or shipping receipt, when applicable
- Itemized invoice or other proof of merchandise value
- Delivery record and any exception notation
- Itemized list of affected goods, quantity, unit value, and requested amount
- For damage: photos of the item as packed, close-up of the shipping label, and outside package damage; package dimensions; retain contents and packaging for possible inspection
- Customer-confirmed carrier terms and source URL

**Packet fields:** shipment/order ID: ___; tracking/reference: ___; scheduled delivery date: ___; issue: ___; evidence paths: ___; source checked on: ___; customer filing date/confirmation: ___; next response check: ___
