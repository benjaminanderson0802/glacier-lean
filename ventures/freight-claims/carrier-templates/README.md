# Carrier claim packet templates

These checklists support a customer-reviewed draft packet. They do not submit a claim, decide coverage, or replace the carrier's current service terms. Confirm the carrier, service, issue type, shipment dates, and source terms before relying on a deadline. Keep the checked source URL and date with the packet.

Each packet should identify the shipment/order, carrier and service, tracking or PRO number, ship and delivery dates, affected items and customer-confirmed quantities, requested amount, evidence paths, and the customer-confirmed claim filing and carrier-response dates. Preserve the original packaging and goods when the carrier may inspect them.

- [UPS](ups.md)
- [FedEx](fedex.md)
- [USPS](usps.md)
- [LTL / Carmack](ltl-carmack.md)

For LTL claims, response reminders are calculated from the filing date entered by the shipper. For parcel carriers, record actual carrier notices and dates; these templates do not promise a response time.
