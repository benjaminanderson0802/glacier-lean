# FedEx packet checklist

## Deadline

FedEx's parcel claim page states: U.S. damage or missing-contents claims, no later than 60 calendar days from shipment date; international damage or missing-contents claims, 21 calendar days from shipment date; lost or undelivered claims, within nine months from shipment date. Confirm the service, destination, issue, and current terms before relying on a date. FedEx Freight LTL has separate claim guidance; use [LTL / Carmack](ltl-carmack.md) only as a checklist and confirm the actual carrier terms.

**Date anchor:** shipment date for the parcel windows above. **Response:** save the claim/reference and notices; record only response dates actually provided by FedEx.

## Evidence

- Tracking number, shipment/order record, and airbill, pickup record, or shipping-system record when available
- Bill of lading or shipping receipt, when applicable
- Original vendor invoice, retail receipt, online order proof of payment, or other proof of value
- Delivery record and any exception notation
- Itemized affected goods, quantities, values, and requested amount
- For damage: photos of the package and contents; keep original packaging until resolved
- When applicable: itemized repair invoice, statement of non-repair, appraisal, expense statement, and serial numbers
- Customer-confirmed service terms and source URL

**Packet fields:** shipment/order ID: ___; tracking/reference: ___; shipment date: ___; domestic/international: ___; issue: ___; evidence paths: ___; source checked on: ___; customer filing date/confirmation: ___; next response check: ___

Source: [FedEx file a claim](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/customer-support/claims.html)
