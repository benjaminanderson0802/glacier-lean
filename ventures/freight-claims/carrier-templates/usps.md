# USPS packet checklist

## Deadline

For domestic damage or missing contents, USPS says to file immediately and no later than 60 days from mailing. Lost-article windows depend on mail class and service; check the current Domestic Mail Manual 609 chart and confirm the applicable earliest and latest filing dates. Do not calculate a lost-mail deadline from a generic 60-day rule.

**Date anchor:** mailing date, after confirming mail class and domestic/international service. **Response:** save the claim/reference and USPS notices; record actual response dates and appeal windows from the notice.

## Evidence

- Tracking number, shipment/order record, and mailing receipt or label record
- Proof of purchased insurance or eligible service
- Proof of value, such as a paid invoice or sales receipt
- Delivery record and any exception notation
- Itemized affected goods, quantities, values, and requested amount
- For damage: photos showing the damage and repair estimate when applicable; retain the item, container, packaging, and contents for possible inspection
- Customer-confirmed mail class, service, destination, and source URL

**Packet fields:** shipment/order ID: ___; tracking/reference: ___; mailing date: ___; mail class/service: ___; domestic/international: ___; issue: ___; evidence paths: ___; source checked on: ___; customer filing date/confirmation: ___; next response check: ___
