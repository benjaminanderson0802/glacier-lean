# LTL / Carmack packet checklist

## Deadline

If Carmack applies, federal law sets a minimum nine-month period for filing a written claim, measured from delivery or, if the carrier failed to deliver, a reasonable time for delivery. The bill of lading, tariff, claim context, and applicable law control the actual deadline. Confirm applicability and the governing terms with the shipper; this estimate is not legal advice.

After the shipper confirms the filing date, track a written acknowledgment within 30 days and a disposition within 120 days. If the claim remains unresolved, request a status update every 60 days. These are response checks, not automated carrier contact or a promise of resolution.

## Evidence

- Written claim identifying the shipment, asserting carrier liability, and stating a determinable amount
- Signed bill of lading and delivery receipt / proof of delivery, including noted exceptions
- Freight bill and proof of freight charges
- Itemized invoice or proof of value, with affected quantity, unit value, and calculation
- NMFC item number, freight class, commodity description, packaging, and tendered weight
- Dated photos of goods, packaging, pallet, and labels; inspection report and salvage/repair estimate when available
- Carrier correspondence, claim receipt/reference, and customer-confirmed filing/response dates
- Any separate insurance record and the shipper's explicit claim request when covered
