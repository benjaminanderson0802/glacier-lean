# start here

Do each venture only after its linked steps are complete. Time is an estimate for the owner’s setup work; account reviews and legal answers can take longer. Glacier prepares drafts and checks. You approve anything that spends money, creates an account, publishes, or is filed with a government agency.

| Venture | What Glacier does automatically | Your 1–3 steps | Time | What this unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CPSC data prep | Checks batch fields against CPSC rules and prepares a registry file. | [Get the lawyer’s written opinion](https://www.cpsc.gov/efiling/importers); [review and certify each batch](https://www.cpsc.gov/efiling/importers); [approve the first broker contract](https://www.cpsc.gov/eFiling-Document-Library). | 30–60 min, plus legal review | Release of a reviewed batch file for you or your broker to submit. |
| Apify data tools (parallel) | Checks Mississippi contractor license status and expiration from the public lookup. | [Set up an Apify developer account](https://console.apify.com/account/integrations); [deploy the prepared checker](https://docs.apify.com/cli/docs/installation); [review and publish the first listing](https://console.apify.com/actors). | 30–60 min, plus account review | Pay-per-use checks after you publish the Actor. |
| Warranty registration | Tracks warranty deadlines and prepares registration work. | [Connect a Jobber test account](https://developer.getjobber.com/); [check each brand’s portal terms](https://www.trane.com/residential/en/resources/warranty-and-registration/); [review the first listing and email drafts](https://developer.getjobber.com/). | 1–2 hours, plus app review | Read-only Jobber checks and reviewed homeowner drafts; brand portal automation remains gated by each brand’s terms. |
| Recall checker | Checks reseller inventory against public recall notices and prepares source-linked pages. | [Review the first 50 pages](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls); [submit the extension listing](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole); [set test prices and review plan wording](https://dashboard.stripe.com/test/products). | 1–2 hours, plus store review | Recall checks and pages ready for your review; public publishing and paid plans stay off until enabled. |
| Co-op claims + street postcards | Checks claim evidence and deadlines; prepares claim drafts and postcard proofs. | [Connect Jobber](https://developer.getjobber.com/); [review the first brand claim](https://www.jobber.com/); [choose routes and approve the postcard proof](https://eddm.usps.com/). | 1–2 hours | Dealer intake, reviewed claim drafts, and locally prepared postcard proofs. |
| Freight claims | Prepares source-linked loss, damage, or shortage packets and tracks carrier response dates. | [Connect ShipStation](https://docs.shipstation.com/); [review the first ten packets](https://www.shipstation.com/); have each shipper [authorize and send its claim](https://www.shipstation.com/). | 1–2 hours | Daily shipment checks and claim packets for the shipper to review and send. |
| FDA cosmetics | Checks product packets against FDA fields and prepares listing files from confirmed product details. | [Connect Shopify and provide label details](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps); [review and submit the FDA listing](https://direct.fda.gov/); [review the Shopify app](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch). | 1–2 hours, plus app review | Checked files for you to submit and a reviewed app listing. |
| OSHA filing | Not installed yet; no OSHA flow is available in Glacier. | No setup steps are available yet. | — | Nothing until the OSHA venture is built and independently checked. |
| Property tax appeals | Prepares evidence packets and postcard proofs from public records and your documents. | [Confirm Cook County scope and counsel’s guidance](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/official-appeal-rules-cook-county-assessor); [register before Texas consulting](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/ptc/); [review the first proof and recipient list](https://www.cookcountyassessor.com/appeals). | 1–2 hours | County-scoped packets and local proofs; Texas work stays off until registration is confirmed. |
| Utility audits | Calculates a customer-reviewed Indiana electricity exemption estimate and unsigned application data. | [Confirm Indiana as the launch state](https://www.in.gov/dor/business-tax/sales-tax/); [provide sales, meter, and bill records](https://www.in.gov/dor/business-tax/sales-tax/); review, sign, and submit the application yourself. | 30–60 min per business | A checked estimate and application draft for your review and submission. |
| DIBBS government supply | Ranks public solicitations and prepares quote files from your authorized supplier quotes. | [Complete SAM.gov and CAGE registration](https://sam.gov/entity-registration); [request authorized supplier quotes and approve spending](https://www.dibbs.bsm.dla.mil/); sign and submit each bid yourself. | 1–2 hours, plus registration | Ranked bid sheets and solicitation-specific files ready for your signature. |
| Carpenter’s goods | Prepares Etsy and Shopify listing drafts and tracks commissions from your exports. | [Agree the commission in writing](https://www.etsy.com/sell); [review and publish the first listing](https://admin.shopify.com/); [provide product and sales records](https://www.etsy.com/sell). | 30–60 min | Reviewed listing drafts and commission summaries from your records. |
| Tariff estimator | On hold; no estimator flow is installed. | No setup steps until counsel approves work on this venture. | — | Nothing while the legal hold remains. |

For connected-account keys, open **your steps** in Glacier and paste each key into the named secret field there. Do not put keys in flow inputs, email, or this document. The Jobber, Shopify, and ShipStation connections still need their one-time secret setup before their live reads can run.
