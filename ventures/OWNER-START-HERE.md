# start here

Owner guide for every in-scope venture in the [Venture Build Spec](SPEC.md), updated with the Whop clipping card on 2026-10-11. **Live** means operating publicly with live-use evidence. **Ready** means the workflow and its checks are prepared, with only owner setup or review left. **Blocked** means a build, source, evidence, or owner decision still prevents launch. No venture is live. Fixture passes, local runs, and connected test accounts are not live customer evidence.

Glacier prepares checks, drafts, reminders, and packets. You or the customer still create accounts, verify identity or payouts, choose prices, approve spending and mail, sign, file, and publish. Use Glacier Settings → Secrets for requested credentials; never put credentials in a flow input or this guide.

## Your next steps, all ventures

These are the remaining owner actions, deduplicated where one setup applies to several ventures. Estimates are hands-on time; platform review, verification, source recovery, legal advice, and waiting for responses can add time. The first items follow the current priority on consistent marketplace and government-bid income.

### DIBBS government supply

1. Complete the pending IRS check, wait for the CAGE code, then finish [DIBBS registration](dibbs-supply/README.md) (estimate: 20–45 minutes of entry time; agency processing is additional).
2. For each bid, verify the solicitation and request authorized supplier quotes in writing, approve any parts spending, then sign and submit it personally in DIBBS (estimate: 30–90 minutes per bid, depending on the quote packet). See [DIBBS](dibbs-supply/README.md).

### eBay and Depop

1. Verify eBay seller/payout details and set up developer credentials and OAuth in one sitting (estimate: 25–45 minutes); then submit the three written supplier applications in a separate sitting (about 25–35 minutes). See [eBay owner steps](ebay-wholesale/OWNER-STEPS.md).
