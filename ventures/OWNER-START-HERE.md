## known limits

- **CPSC:** all three registered government sources returned HTTP 403 in the independent review, so no live flagged-code, rule-code, or registry-template data was retrieved. A draft is not a release-ready registry file until those feeds and their canaries pass.
- **Apify:** only the Mississippi lookup has live canary evidence. A reusable second state/board and its separate-engine review are not built; no public Actor listing has been published.
- **Apify publication gate:** the review-required approval record for the first three distinct Actor publications is not implemented; only the first listing is currently in the owner steps. No second board is included or represented as supported.
- **Warranty:** Jobber’s connector does not yet prove the full completed-install/homeowner/unit field contract. There are no source-backed brand serial patterns, so real brand records remain uncertain and portal automation stays off. Email drafts and a live listing have not been demonstrated.
- **Warranty evidence:** a complete end-to-end read-only Jobber → reader/deadline → homeowner draft loop has not been demonstrated; there are no eligible test accounts or real records in this check.
- **Recall checker:** the required labeled 500-recalled/500-clean false-no-match benchmark has not been run. FSIS returned HTTP 403 in the review; FDA and NHTSA full-source refreshes did not finish there. The fuzzy/second-engine borderline route and wrong-result feedback loop are not demonstrated. Public hosting and publishing remain manual. The output-vocabulary test conflict is recorded in the recall claim and final audit.
- **Co-op claims:** Jobber dealer intake, live brand rules, reader/deadline integration, production mail, and a real claim were not demonstrated. Mail remains test/render-only; checkout and actual EDDM sending are not enabled.
- **Freight claims:** no live ShipStation account or carrier claim was available. Intake is local; no hosted upload link is active. The shipper still authorizes and sends claims, and settlement is a customer decision.
- **FDA cosmetics:** no live Shopify catalog, brand label, Cosmetics Direct response, or FDA acceptance was verified. Response forwarding still depends on the customer. Retention deletion and customer-layer billing remain launch dependencies.
- **OSHA filing:** the preparation flow is a worksheet, not an automatic certification or submission. No customer injury log or OSHA account was used; the establishment executive must certify, submit, and post the summary.
- **Property tax:** Cook County’s representation-rule discrepancy keeps customer launch disabled until the owner resolves it. The 2026 roll sync is staged in bounded pages, not complete. Texas is future expansion only.
- **Utility audits:** no customer bills or rates were available. Reachable rate links are marked unverified; the output is an unsigned estimate and application data, not a filing.
- **DIBBS:** the public solicitation feed returned a DoD warning/consent page and zero rows. Do not treat this as an empty market; verify solicitations manually at the linked official page.
- **Carpenter’s goods:** comparables and paid-sale rows must come from owner exports. Buyer replies, order handling, support, inventory updates, and future listing publication remain manual.
- **Installer and release boards:** 34 flows validate, but an existing installer test still assumes the repository contains only two Apify flows. The full backend and UI boards are recorded separately in the final audit; a passing integration count does not resolve any venture-specific release limit above.
