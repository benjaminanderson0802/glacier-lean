# start here

Owner guide for every in-scope venture in the [Venture Build Spec](SPEC.md), regenerated from all 12 `venture.json` manifests and their READMEs on 2026-10-10. **Live** means operating publicly with live-use evidence. **Ready** means the workflow and its checks are prepared, with only owner setup or review left. **Blocked** means a build, source, evidence, or owner decision still prevents launch. No venture is live. Fixture passes, local runs, and connected test accounts are not live customer evidence.

Glacier prepares checks, drafts, reminders, and packets. You or the customer still create accounts, verify identity or payouts, choose prices, approve spending and mail, sign, file, and publish. Use Glacier Settings → Secrets for requested credentials; never put credentials in a flow input or this guide.

## Known limits and remaining test failures

Both checks remain unchanged. An earlier combined pytest collection also encountered generic `src`/`scripts` import collisions; the installer discovery suite passes in the latest run.
