# Co-op claims + street postcards

This venture reads completed Jobber jobs through the same read-only shared `JobberClient` used by warranty, then creates local co-op claim drafts with a source-linked brand record, evidence checklist, and PDF/Markdown output. It does not open a brand portal or file claims. The connected Jobber account name is used as the dealer label; homeowner/client names are not requested or copied into drafts. The postcard flow prepares a local proof for review and never mails it.

The postcard half creates distinct local landing pages and a two-sided, render-only EDDM Retail flat proof. It checks the sender, do-not-mail list, address completeness, and USPS size/weight limits before rendering. The local EDDM proof PDF uses an 11.5 × 6.25 inch page box; it is a review proof, not a production master, and has no postal indicia, bleed, paper-stock or color-profile settings. No Lob request, print order, publication, or mailing is made.

Brand records live in `brand-rules.json`. Carrier, Lennox, Rheem, and Goodman have public program or marketing-support pages, but the current account-specific eligibility, evidence, expense, and claim-deadline terms still require owner confirmation. Trane's public dealer page points to its secure dealer portal for details. All five records therefore say `unverified`; these records are source pointers and checklist starters, not program-rule approvals. If a confirmed program expiry is supplied in the local job detail, the shared deadline tracker registers 60-day and 30-day reminders. Without that owner-confirmed date, the draft says deadline pending and schedules nothing.

`workflow.BRAND_RULES` exposes the five source-linked records by lowercase brand name, with explicit `status` and `verified` aliases for callers. `prepare_claim_draft(job, output_dir=...)` adapts compact Jobber-shaped records into local draft artifacts. The claim intake query requests only job identifiers, title/status, completion date and source URL; it does not request client, property, or custom-field data. Drafts retain only claim-relevant fields. Missing claim evidence and unverified program terms keep the result not filing-ready. The compact-input acceptance check disputes that readiness result in `DISPUTE.md`.

The shared rules checker and document reader remain available for later evidence review. Current claim drafts do not imply that extracted documents prove eligibility; missing items stay unchecked. No brand credentials or portal access are needed by Glacier, and the claim filing script is now a no-op safety guard. The Jobber test app is connected for owner use, but its OAuth credentials were not available in this sandbox, so live account intake remains unverified here.

## Your step

Connect the read-only Jobber test app in Glacier Settings → Secrets (create/configure the app in [Jobber Developer Center](https://developer.getjobber.com/) if needed); confirm your current brand eligibility, claim terms and deadlines; then authorize, sign and file claims yourself, choose any price or campaign budget, and approve the exact postcard proof before arranging a mailing through [USPS EDDM](https://eddm.usps.com/). Glacier prepares local drafts and proofs only.

## Billing and limits

The spec's starting estimate is a 15–25% fee on co-op funds after payout and about $550 per shared-card ad spot, paid at checkout. Pricing and checkout are not activated. If a route card does not fill, the intended policy is refund or roll forward. No paid mailing service is used by this flow.

Dealer and buyer content stays local. Use street-level completed-job addresses only, never homeowner names. Automated 30-day upload deletion is not implemented yet; do not put live customer files into this partial workflow until retention is wired in.

The draft runner is `python3 ventures/coop-postcards/scripts/generate_claim_drafts.py`. It reads completed-job metadata from Jobber and writes local `.md`, `.pdf`, and `.json` files under `GLACIER_HOME/ventures/coop-postcards/claims/`. For fixture-only offline rendering, pass `--fixture ventures/coop-postcards/fixtures/jobber_completed_job.json --output-dir <local-directory>`. It ignores non-completed jobs. If the Jobber test app is not already connected in Glacier, run this one-command owner check from the repository root after saving its four OAuth values in Settings → Secrets: `~/w/glacier-lean/.venv/bin/python ventures/coop-postcards/scripts/generate_claim_drafts.py`. It reads only completed-job metadata and writes review drafts locally. `run_approved_claim.py` intentionally does not submit anything. After personally filing, record the brand response with `scripts/record_claim_outcome.py --claim-id <id> --owner-filed --outcome accepted|rejected --reason "<brand response>"`; a first rejection allows one corrected draft, and a second rejection marks it dropped. Glacier never resubmits.
