# Cook County filing path decision

**Decision for this card:** enable a homeowner self-file assist for an individual taxpayer filing for themselves. Glacier prepares the evidence packet and a source-linked checklist. The homeowner chooses the filing stage, confirms the current deadline, reviews the records, signs, submits, and tracks the case. Glacier does not act as a representative or access either county portal.

| Path | What it means | Benefits | Limits and owner work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Homeowner self-file assist** | Glacier prepares a reviewed evidence packet and separate steps for the Assessor and Board of Review. The homeowner files personally, pro se. | Matches the official rules for an individual taxpayer; keeps filing, signatures, portal accounts, and judgment with the homeowner; no attorney account or representation is implied. | Homeowner checks township windows and current requirements, decides whether and where to appeal, signs and submits, saves receipts, and follows notices. Board self-representation applies when the property is titled in that individual's own name; entity-owned property requires an attorney. |
| **B. Partner with a licensed attorney** | A licensed attorney reviews or represents the homeowner and files where authorized. | Adds legal judgment and representation; suitable if the homeowner wants help or title/ownership falls outside the Board's individual pro se rule. | Requires finding and approving a licensed partner, engagement terms, fees, conflict checks, and secure document sharing. Glacier must not describe an unlicensed service as the customer's representative. This is not needed to enable the homeowner's own filing. |
| **C. Assessor-stage only** | Prepare an Assessor packet and guide, with no Board of Review workflow. | Smaller scope; Assessor rules expressly allow pro se filing, and the current SmartFile system is the direct filing route. | Does not support a later Board appeal path. The homeowner still needs to follow township deadlines and decide whether to pursue further review. It would leave a common next stage outside Glacier's guide. |

**Why A for this launch:** The official Assessor rules say no party needs an attorney or agent to file there and direct pro se filers to SmartFile. The Board's rules allow an individual taxpayer to represent property titled in their own name pro se, while requiring entities and anyone representing another taxpayer to use a licensed attorney. This supports a narrow self-file workflow for individual homeowners; it does not support Glacier filing or representing customers. The Assessor and Board have separate township-specific filing windows, so the guide links to live calendars instead of embedding dates. The owner-approved scope is therefore homeowner self-file assist, with attorney partnership as an optional future service and no automatic filing.

This is a product-scope decision grounded in the linked office rules, not legal advice. Before each filing, the homeowner should confirm current rules and dates with the relevant office. The guide does not promise an appeal outcome. Paid checkout stays disabled until the owner confirms pricing. Live mailing stays disabled; postcard proofs remain local.

## Official sources

- [Cook County Assessor, Official Appeal Rules](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/official-appeal-rules-cook-county-assessor) — 2026 rules; no attorney representation requirement, SmartFile guidance, and alternate routes for pro se filers without internet access.
- [Cook County Assessor, Residential Appeals](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/residential-appeals) — residential scope, supporting records, and current online filing guidance.
- [Cook County Assessor, Assessment Calendar and Deadlines](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/assessment-calendar-and-deadlines) — separate township appeal dates; current calendar page last updated October 8, 2026 when reviewed for this memo.
- [Cook County Board of Review, Official Rules](https://www.cookcountyboardofreview.com/board-review-official-rules) — Rule 1 pro se rule and title restriction, Rules 7–8 township deadlines and filing routes, and Rule 16 signature requirement; page states rules last updated July 27, 2026.
- [Cook County Board of Review, Residential Appeals](https://www.cookcountyboardofreview.com/assessment-appeals/residential) — individual taxpayer self-representation, entity attorney requirement, and township closing dates.
