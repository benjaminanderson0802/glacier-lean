# Pilot pitch

**Subject:** A one-month public works bid digest for Mobile and Baldwin County contractors

Hi,

I’m testing a simple weekly digest that filters public works notices for general contractors working in Mobile and Baldwin Counties. Each item includes the issuing authority, county, listed deadline, pre-bid meeting when available, and a direct link to the official notice. If a field is missing, the digest says to check the source.

Would a sample based on notices your team already receives save you time? I’m offering one 30-day pilot for **$49**, with one weekly digest. You keep control of your registrations and bids, and the issuing authority’s notice remains the source of truth. This is a discovery aid, not a complete bid list or a promise that an opportunity fits.

If you’re interested, reply with the counties and kinds of work you pursue. You can forward an official notice email or share an official export/link for a sample; please do not send account credentials or private bid information.

Thank you,

Benjamin Anderson
Anderson Education LLC

## Paid-pilot offer

- 30 days, one weekly digest, general contracting, Mobile and Baldwin Counties.
- Test price: $49 for the 30-day pilot; cancel by simply not continuing.
- Customer supplies official notice emails, official exports, or links. No credential collection, scraping, or portal filing.
- Every item links back to the issuing authority; the customer checks deadlines, addenda, eligibility, and bid instructions.
- The owner handles any conversation, agreement, billing, and customer-facing email personally.

## Stop rule

Do not continue past the sample if fewer than **three firms commit to the paid pilot**, or if none of the reviewers can identify a useful match or time saved. If the rule fails, pause the offer and reconsider the niche; do not expand coverage or claim completeness to improve the numbers.
