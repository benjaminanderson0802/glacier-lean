# Owner steps — carpenter goods

The preparation files are ready. There are **2 owner sittings**: (1) agree/sign and provide the maker-confirmed inputs, then (2) review and publish the drafts if approved. Glacier never signs or publishes. Do not use synthetic test fixtures as product, price, or sales evidence.

## 1. Meet with the carpenter: complete/sign terms and provide the source files

**Time:** about 30–60 minutes, plus however long it takes the carpenter to locate photos and records.

1. Open [`agreement/commission-agreement.md`](agreement/commission-agreement.md) and [`agreement/commission-summary.md`](agreement/commission-summary.md). Fill legal names and addresses, rate, exact eligible amount, treatment of shipping/tax/fees/discounts/refunds/returns/chargebacks, duties, permissions, notice, and payment timing together. The choices are left open where the owner and carpenter must decide; do not sign with blanks or unchecked alternatives.
2. Attach the completed maker-confirmed product sheet. Copy [`templates/product-sheet-template.csv`](templates/product-sheet-template.csv) to `$GLACIER_HOME/ventures/carpenter-goods/incoming/product-sheet.csv`; replace its blank row using the exact requested facts in [`OWNER-DATA-REQUEST.md`](OWNER-DATA-REQUEST.md). Keep the photos local and put their local paths in `photos`, separated by semicolons.
3. Put at least three current, completed, source-linked sales for the same product category in `$GLACIER_HOME/ventures/carpenter-goods/incoming/comparables.json`, using the prefilled shape in the data request. Use actual sale pages/records, not asking prices. If there are fewer than three eligible records, Glacier leaves the suggested price blank and marks it **uncertain — please check**.
4. Both parties sign the final agreement themselves and keep a copy. If the commission flow will be used now, after signature copy its agreed terms into `$GLACIER_HOME/ventures/carpenter-goods/incoming/commission-terms.json` using the schema in the data request. For existing sales, download the relevant seller order exports only after agreeing who may access and retain them:
   - [Etsy Shop Manager](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) → **Settings** → **Options** → **Download Data** → **Orders** → choose **CSV Type**, **Month**, and **Year** → **Download CSV**. Save the Etsy order CSV locally as `incoming/etsy-orders.csv`. [Etsy's official export instructions](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000343328-How-to-Download-a-Spreadsheet-of-Your-Sold-Transactions).
   - [Shopify Admin](https://admin.shopify.com/) → **Orders** → **Export** → choose the relevant orders/date range → **Export as: CSV** → **Export orders**. Save the CSV locally as `incoming/shopify-orders.csv`. For exports over 50 orders or by date, Shopify emails a download link to the owner. [Shopify's official export instructions and CSV fields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/exporting-orders).
5. Before the first physical-goods sale, review [Georgia DOR's tax registration guidance](https://dor.georgia.gov/tax-registration) and [Sales & Use Tax registration instructions](https://dor.georgia.gov/how-register-sales-and-use-tax-account). Applicability is **uncertain — please check**; this guide does not determine it. If registration applies, open [Georgia Tax Center](https://gtc.dor.ga.gov/): for a new business choose **Register a New Georgia Business** → **Next** → choose the correct business type (sole proprietors skip that screen) → complete the requested business, address, responsible-party, and login/contact fields → review → **Submit** → **OK**. For an existing business, log in → **More…** → **Register a New Tax Account** → **Sales & Use Tax** → **Next** → complete location/address and account questions → **Submit** → **OK**. Enter values directly from the owner's official business records; no tax ID or personal identifier is prefilled in this local request file. Do not call the department as part of this prep workflow.

## 2. Review the prepared drafts and publish only if you approve

**Time:** about 15–30 minutes for the first product, depending on the photos and shop fields.

1. Run **Prepare a handmade furniture listing** in Glacier after the product sheet and comparable records are saved. Review the resulting local `drafts/listing.json` and the human-readable [Etsy draft](drafts/etsy-listing-draft.md) and [Shopify draft](drafts/shopify-listing-draft.md). Confirm every product fact and photo with the maker. Confirm the suggested price against the source-linked sales and make the final price choice yourself. Do not continue if the result says uncertain.
2. To create the Etsy listing, open [Etsy Shop Manager](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) → **Listings** → **Add a listing**. Confirm the item fits Etsy's current seller policy, copy the reviewed values, and add the original photos. Keep it unpublished until you approve; on the final page choose **Publish** only if you are ready for it to go live (Etsy says a listing can also be saved as a draft).
3. To create the Shopify listing, open [Shopify Admin](https://admin.shopify.com/) → **Products** → **Add product**. Copy the reviewed title and description, add the original photos, set the owner-approved price and availability, select **Draft** status, then **Save**. To make it live later, reopen the product, change **Status** to **Active**, and **Save** only when you approve publication.

Shop accounts, billing, seller verification, shop policies, taxes, shipping/pickup arrangements, and all publication actions remain under the account owner's control. No cold outreach or calls are part of this venture. Customer exports and photos stay local; remove customer exports within 30 days after the commission job closes unless the customer asks to keep them.

For later commission periods, run **Track agreed furniture commissions** after saving the signed terms and current seller exports, then use Glacier's review prompt to compare the actual report with platform settlements before payment. This is the required human verification step for each calculation, not another launch setup step.
