# Launch state decision: Indiana

## Recommendation

Use **Indiana** as the first launch state, subject to Benjamin's confirmation before customer work. Indiana has two narrow paths that match this venture: restaurants may elect a 50% exemption on electricity through a single meter using ST-200R, while other qualifying customers can submit a utility study with ST-200. Indiana DOR decides eligibility and issues any exemption certificate.

For the utility-study path, Bulletin #55 is the directly applicable utility bulletin. It says a meter qualifies for the 100% utility sales-tax exemption only when exempt uses exceed 50%, evaluated meter by meter; Form ST-200 is used to apply. A study estimate is not a DOR determination. If the predominant-use threshold is not met, other partial production exemptions may apply and require separate professional review. Bulletin #29 is still relevant to the restaurant path because its Appendix B explains the 75% prepared-food sales calculation. It is not the governing bulletin for utility studies. Bulletin #64, effective October 1, 2026, separately discusses production exemptions and helps define qualifying production activity.

## Alternatives

| State | Relevant rule | Launch tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minnesota | Utilities used in qualifying industrial or agricultural production may be exempt. Mixed-use meters generally require an energy study that documents exempt and taxable uses, equipment consumption, and meter usage. The purchaser gives the supplier Form ST3. | Has an energy-study path, but its production rules exclude restaurants and focus on production use beyond average climate control or lighting. There is no matching Indiana-style 50% restaurant election. |
| Texas | Mixed-use natural gas or electricity on one meter is treated as wholly exempt or taxable based on predominant use. The required study covers twelve consecutive months and must be certified by a registered engineer or a person with an engineering degree; the business owner also certifies equipment and hours. | Strong fit for a study workflow, but stricter study certification increases launch burden. Scope needs a qualified study preparer and Texas-specific forms and guidance. |
